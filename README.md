Shadow Gallery — a top-down stealth game set in the Marrow Museum after dark. You are Wren, a cat burglar with one night to steal the Star Sapphire and two relics from each of three wings, then slip out the north door unseen. Guards patrol with visible vision cones: break their line of sight behind display cases, statues and crates, crouch in the shadows where they spot you slowly, and toss a pebble to pull a guard off his route. Get seen and the alarm rises; get caught three times and the night is over. Three wings, a best-score chase, and synth sounds — all offline, in your browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with WASD or the arrow keys (Shift to sneak, E or click to toss a pebble, P to pause, M to mute).

## Stealth tips

- **Shadows are safety.** A guard needs more than three times as long to spot you in the dark — crouch there while a patrol passes.
- **Sneaking is slower but quieter.** It also halves how fast a guard's suspicion grows.
- **Pebbles pull guards.** Toss one past a patrol and the nearest guard will walk over to look, opening a gap.
- **Watch the arc.** The ring over a guard's head is his suspicion: a `?` means he is getting curious, a `!` means run.
- **The exit only opens** once every artifact in the wing is stolen — an arrow over Wren points the way out.
